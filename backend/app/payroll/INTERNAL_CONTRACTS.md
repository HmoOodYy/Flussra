# Payroll internal contracts

This guide records cross-module helper contracts verified in the current backend source scan. These names are internal implementation contracts, not an external API. Production callers import the owner directly; tests keep their existing consumer-module monkeypatch seams. This guide and the owner module documentation add no aliases or facade.

## Cross-package internal contracts

| Owner | Supported helper | Consumers | Contract |
|---|---|---|---|
| `app.payroll.workflow_lock` | `acquire_branch_workflow_lock(company_id, branch_id, db)` | Payroll lifecycle/creation/finalization, Payroll Setup locks, Review decisions, eligibility/Workforce boundary operations, Driver-owned compensation writes | Takes PostgreSQL's transaction-level advisory lock keyed by `(company_id, branch_id)` on the caller's active connection transaction. It performs no authorization and does not begin, commit, or roll back the caller's transaction. |
| `app.payroll.guards` | `require_no_finalized_payroll(company_id, driver_id, footprint_from, footprint_to, mode, db)` | `compensation.schedules` approval/void; `payroll.driver_pay_rules` create/end/void | Read-only Driver-wide guard through `payroll.fn_DriverFinalizedPayrollTouched`: `Locked`/`Archived` periods with captured eligibility for any assignment of the Driver, in any Branch. Inclusive footprint, `None` unbounded, inverted footprint empty. `WorkDate` tests period overlap; `PeriodStart` tests the period start. Refuses with HTTP 422 / `DRIVER_COMPENSATION_IN_FINALIZED_PERIOD`. Owns no authorization, lock, commit, or caller ordering. |
| `app.payroll.guards` | `driver_payroll_period_branches(company_id, driver_id, from_date, db)` | `compensation.driver_context.lock_driver_compensation_write` | Lock-discovery twin of the finalized guard: both use `payroll.fn_DriverPayrollPeriodsForFootprint` over captured eligibility. Returns Branches of periods of any status overlapping `[from_date, infinity)` in `WorkDate` mode, a superset of `PeriodStart`. Writers lock the union of these Branches and live tenure Branches in ascending ID before row locks and the final authority/guard recheck; a timezone change must not exclude a captured historical period. |

| Owner | Supported helpers | Verified consumers | Invariant to preserve |

Existing-source rescue is exact-date only. `IncludedByExistingData` never grants new period-level Bonus authority. Draft-to-Open regeneration replaces provisional rows and freezes the snapshot. | `app.payroll.day_entry_state` | `_upsert_entry_state`, `lock_selected_status_keys`, `_enforce_status_key_limits` | Day Grid; canonical-entry, Status day-entry and Status-limit tests | The canonical entry state (`PayrollPeriodDriverDayEntryState`, one non-void row per DriverAssignment and WorkDate) is the only Status source. Writes preserve field ownership (`set_status`/`set_note`); a status-only or note-only write leaves the other field untouched. `lock_selected_status_keys` takes the selected Statuses `FOR SHARE` for the transaction, so a concurrent catalog edit of hours, the off-day semantic, Category or rate column either committed before the save or waits and is refused as in use, and a Status deactivated since it was resolved cannot be newly selected. Usage limits are the Branch x Status limits of the Company catalog, counted from canonical selections of the period (never from the legacy `DailyStatus` duplicate) under the Period lock, excluding overwritten batch slots and adding the batch's new contributions separately; "per driver" counts each DriverAssignment separately. |
| `app.payroll.status_work_conflict` | `assert_no_off_status_work_conflicts`, `assert_no_off_status_with_work`, `FinalDayState`, `STATUS_OFF_WORK_CONFLICT` | Day Grid save, draft-line add/update (`draft_line_mutation`); `test_status_off_work_conflict.py` | Full-day-off integrity: a Status with `IsOffReason` cannot share a DriverAssignment day with a non-void ordinary DraftLine of quantity > 0. The Day Grid judges the final state of every row (resolved Status and the ordinary quantities left, unsubmitted definitions keeping their quantity) under the Period lock and refuses the whole batch with HTTP 422 `STATUS_OFF_WORK_CONFLICT`; its quantity writes run in two passes (clearing before the Status write, setting after) so every intermediate database state is valid. The source-line writers refuse new work on an off day. The database repeats the rule (`payroll.fn_guard_status_off_work`, triggers on `PayrollDraftLines` and `PayrollPeriodDriverDayEntryState`) behind a per-(period, assignment, date) advisory lock, so writers that bypass the Period lock are serialized too. That slot is also what an UPDATE can change (none of its three columns is immutable on either table), so the triggers fire on a change of `PayrollPeriodID`, `DriverAssignmentID` or `WorkDate` as well, and judge the destination slot exactly as an insert there. A day-entry write that references a Status also holds that Status's shared advisory lock (`payroll.fn_StatusKeyLockKey`) until it commits, and a semantic edit of the Status takes it exclusively before it looks for references, so an edit cannot race a first use past the in-use check. |
| `app.payroll.status_calculation` | `load_live_status_lines`, `LiveStatusLine` | `period_calculation` (live packet) | Direct Status compensation, read-only: each non-void canonical selection whose Status is bound to a Status rate column is paid `HoursValue x` the rate of the permanent Driver's schedule of the column's scalar RateDefinition effective on the WorkDate, resolved set-wise through `compensation.resolver.resolve_many` and calculated by the same per-unit kernel as an ordinary line (`definition_calculation.calculate_scalar_rate`). A Status without a rate column is non-monetary and never a blocker; no schedule is MISSING_RATE and a schedule without its value INCOMPLETE_RATE (both blockers); an explicit zero is a calculated 0. It performs no INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE: no `STATUS_PAYMENT` DraftLine is written, refreshed or read as financial authority by any path, and nothing is copied at a transfer because the schedule follows the Driver. |
| `app.settings.status_keys`, `app.settings.status_rate_columns`, `app.settings.status_key_limits` | `resolve_status_input`, `create_status_key`, `update_status_key`, `ensure_default_status_rate_column`, `lock_status_catalog`, `list_branch_limits`, `put_branch_limits` | Day Grid, settings routes, `scripts/ensure_dev_admin.py` | The Company Status catalog. Names and aliases share one normal form (NFKC, trim, collapse, casefold); a Status is resolved from typed text as primary name, then alias, then technical code, and must match exactly one active Status (`STATUS_NOT_FOUND`, `STATUS_INPUT_AMBIGUOUS`). A selection stores the Status, never the alias. A grid row names its Status either by `status_key_id` (the canonical identity the grid returned, used as is through `get_status_by_id`, with no text read) or by newly typed `status_key` text; the technical code of the Status the day already holds names that Status ahead of the name and alias lookups, so an unrelated save never re-reads the held Status as another Status's alias. After use (a selection or frozen Status evidence references it) hours, the off-day semantic, Category and rate column cannot change (409 `STATUS_KEY_IN_USE`). Every catalog writer takes the Company catalog advisory lock first. A Company has exactly one default active Status rate column, healed by the column-create and Status-create writers and the dev seed through the idempotent `payroll.fn_EnsureDefaultStatusRateColumn`; no read path writes. Branch limits live in `PayrollStatusKeyBranchLimits` (Branch x Status). |
| `app.payroll.status_evidence` | `status_entries_from_content`, `status_evidence_availability`, `finalized_status_evidence`, `report_evidence_from_content` | Day Grid, Off Drivers and Drivers Off reads of Locked/Archived periods | Status evidence of a finalized period is read only from the verified snapshot: each entry freezes the Status identity, canonical name, off semantic, hours and Category. EMPTY is a positive fact; evidence that does not verify is UNAVAILABLE with a stable reason and fabricates no row. |
| `app.payroll.bonus` | `_load_active_bonus_events` | Live Calculation packet and report-evidence capture; `report_read_model` LIVE path | Selects only Active canonical `PayrollBonusEvents` for the period and company on the caller's existing connection; retains the driver/employee and creator display joins, ordered `(driverassignmentid, payrollbonuseventid)` rows, and raw mapping objects. It opens no transaction and does not authorize or project the rows. |
| `app.payroll.definition_calculation` | `calculate_definition_input`, `calculate_scalar_rate`, `is_method_operational`, `quantity_error`, `DefinitionCalculation` | `live_source_calculation`, `status_calculation`, period creation, Day Grid, draft-line mutation | The one method-owned calculation boundary. It receives the frozen period definition, the WHOLE resolved `DriverRateSchedule` and a quantity; consumers never branch on the calculation method. Dispatch is keyed by the frozen `(method, version)`: `(PerUnit, 1)` multiplies the one scalar value (CP-4A kernel) and `(OrdinalTier, 1)` pays each ordinal unit of a whole-number quantity with the value of the tier covering it (`payroll/calculation/ordinal_tier.py`; tiers 1=3, 2=4, 3+=5 and quantity 4 pay 17, exact at internal precision, ordinals restarting per line); every other pair is `METHOD_NOT_READY`. No schedule is `MISSING_RATE`, a schedule with an absent, negative or malformed value set is `INCOMPLETE_RATE`, a quantity the method cannot accept is `INVALID_QUANTITY`, an explicit zero is a calculated 0. A single-value method reports `rate_amount`; a multi-value method reports `rate_values` (every tier value) and `tier_contributions`, and no single rate. |
| `app.compensation.ordinal_topology` | `topology_error` | Generic PayDefinition governance (submission, approval, direct creation), the OrdinalTier calculator and the evidence verifier | The one validation of an OrdinalTier topology: at least one tier, the first tier starts at 1, positive integer bounds, each tier starts right after the previous one ends (no gap, no overlap), exactly the last tier is open-ended. Pure; the database repeats it with the overlap exclusion and the structure completeness check. |
| `app.payroll.period_calculation` | `_build_live_calculation_packet` | Period lifecycle, current hub, report read model; `test_cp5a_current_hub.py` patches the existing qualified builder seam | Builds the authoritative live packet and blockers without persistence. `_LiveCalculationPacket` is defined here and imported by `calculation_snapshot` for snapshot-writer typing; other packet dataclasses remain local. From DD3 the packet carries Company currency, official components (`payable-rounding-v1`) and Min/Max proration (`minmax-proration-v1`). Ordinary earnings are summed per DriverAssignment × PayDefinition × period, Status per DriverAssignment × status rate column × period, Bonus per DriverAssignment × period, and each rule adjustment separately; each component is rounded once with `round_payable` (`ROUND_HALF_EVEN`, stored currency digits). Line amounts retain internal precision; Driver totals sum official components without re-rounding. Min/Max uses the official ordinary + Status base, excludes Bonus, selects Driver-owned rules at period start and checks the home calendar, then prorates by frozen eligible scheduled days / scheduled days with D1 date ownership and Off reasons excluded. No day rows blocks Min/Max (`MINMAX_PERIOD_DAYS_MISSING`); zero scheduled days warns and skips rules, zero eligible days skips without warning. The packet retains raw/official values, rule/calendar context and versions that `calculation_snapshot` freezes unchanged. `payroll/calculation/payable_rounding.py` is the only currency-rounding function. |
| `app.payroll.calculation_snapshot` | `_capture_calculation_snapshot` | Period lifecycle submit/resubmit | Freezes the calculation packet and the captured report evidence in the caller-owned transaction as one immutable snapshot (calculation contract `payroll-calculation-v4`, report evidence version 3): header with currency, both rule versions, period dates, frozen scheduled days and warnings; Driver totals with DriverAssignment/Driver/Employee/Branch identity; the official components (kind, target identity, pre-rounding and official amount); one Min/Max evaluation per Driver total with its rule and proration evidence; per-source lines (DraftLine, StatusEntryState, BonusEvent) with target rate provenance (a Status line freezes its Status rate column, RateDefinition and exact Driver schedule, with the hours as its quantity); the used-rate evidence (one schedule row per schedule used, owned by a PayDefinition or a Status rate column, never both; a `Scalar` row carries its one component and amount, an `OrdinalTierSchedule` row carries none and its tier values, the ordinal range and value of every tier, are rows of `PayrollCalculationSnapshotUsedRateComponents`; an OrdinalTier line freezes no single rate but its expanded per-tier explanation in `SourceEvidenceJSONB.TierContributions`); and the report evidence (a Status entry freezes hours and Category beside its name and off semantic). It never recalculates, rounds or re-derives money, rejects blockers, and excludes generated IDs from the hash. Before returning it reads the snapshot back through `snapshot_verification.verify_snapshot`, so the hash recomputed from the persisted rows must equal the hash computed from the packet. System Min/Max lines of the packet are not persisted. Submit and Resubmit each call it once and bind the resulting revision to the Pending PeriodApproval review item. |
| `app.payroll.snapshot_verification` | `load_snapshot_content`, `verify_snapshot`, `reconcile_snapshot_content`, `reconcile_used_rate_evidence`, `reconcile_method_evidence`, `reconcile_report_evidence` | Snapshot writer; approval, finalization, finalization preview and every frozen reader (through `financial_authority`) | The one canonical verification of a snapshot, from its persisted rows alone: the financial hash (`snapshot_hash.calculate_snapshot_hash`, which includes the used-rate evidence the lines link to by fingerprint), the evidence arithmetic, every source line belonging to the component of its own identity (not just its kind), every line's agreement with its used-rate row (no orphan rows), owner exclusivity of each used-rate row, the shape of each used-rate row (a scalar row carries one rate, a tiered row a valid topology of non-negative tier values), the method evidence of each ordinary line (an OrdinalTier line is recomputed from its frozen quantity and tiers: its calculation version, amount and per-tier explanation must equal the recomputation), a Status line's quantity equal to its entry's frozen hours and its amount equal to hours times its rate through the per-unit kernel, the frozen hours range (0 to 24) and Category set, the report evidence version and its own hash, and the overlaps between the report evidence and the financial evidence (Bonus events equal the Bonus lines, each Status line is a Status entry, no counted Min/Max day is an off-reason entry, Driver identity). | `app.payroll.finalization` | `finalize_period`, `get_finalization_preview` | Payroll routes | Both load the single Approved PeriodApproval review item of the period, verify its bound snapshot with `verify_snapshot` and project it with `final_line_projection`; neither builds a live packet or reads live compensation.
